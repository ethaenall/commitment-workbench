# Pre-evaluation source/oracle review

This is authoring QA, not a live model-output evaluation, a blinded pilot, or
measured efficacy. It used inherited ASTRA MAX agent reasoning and local
standard-library checks. No inference transport, integration action, or service
call was invoked for evaluation.

- Primary author integrated and read all twenty active source/oracle pairs.
- A separate author reviewed learn-03/04, validate-03/04 and fresh-05–10:
  PASS; no concrete source support, identity, inclusion, date, or uncertainty
  blocker reported. The reviewed files remained unchanged.
- Another author reviewed fresh-14/15/16 (58 messages, 15 items, 28 current/prior
  anchors): PASS. All 9 non-null due instants matched source timezone-bearing
  promises. Named participants, physical delivery versus receipts, day-only
  dates, missing storage replies, limited reopening and the untrusted directive
  were reconciled. Read hashes stayed stable.
- Primary author separately inspected fresh-11/12/13 (55 messages, 15 items):
  PASS. The two Alex identities, accepted/unaccepted changes, direct dependencies,
  physical custody, refund-state contradiction, language-file collisions,
  narrow paper approval and exact-time-to-day-only change are supported.
- Author checks found no shared complete bodies or substantive copied sentences
  against the learning/validation sources. Native verification separately checks
  all source hashes, exact unique oracle anchors, shared output contracts and
  runtime/source registry equality.

Known scoring limits remain explicit: finite title aliases and source-location
checks are an automatic screen, not complete prose entailment. Partial dates,
materiality and reply correctness need mode-blinded semantic review for any
usable-handoff claim. The pilot proposal makes its output-field conventions
shared across every arm; it does not hide them in refinement guidance.

One reviewed convention deserves care: fresh-14's fender-delivery changed:false
is supported because the new size is a quotation and the purchase release has
not issued; an unchanged date alone would not excuse an accepted scope change.

Old learn-01/02, validate-01/02 and fresh-01–04 bytes were not altered. The four
old fresh cases are historical development evidence and are absent from the
new twelve-case fresh denominator. No model output was used to select cases,
weaken an oracle, change thresholds or manufacture improvement.
